video-pipeline

A private production tool for two YouTube channels.

video-pipeline is a command-line tool used by one person to produce and publish videos on their own YouTube channels, copium dev and Arlo. It renders videos on the owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API to upload them and to manage their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and comments.

It is not offered to the public, and nobody other than the channel owner signs in to it.
